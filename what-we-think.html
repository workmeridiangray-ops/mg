04 Industry news

What’s changing, and why it matters.

Selected external news and updates, with a short note on what each means for your business.

  • AI & Search
    Google confirms AEO and GEO rely on classic search signals

    Google's own product leadership has clarified that AI Overviews and AI Mode still depend on standard retrieval and quality signals, now combined with query fan-out and answer synthesis — well-structured, well-sourced content keeps mattering.

    2026
  • AI & Search
    A growing share of searches now end inside an AI answer

    As more queries get answered directly in AI Overviews and chat assistants rather than a list of links, being citable — not just rankable — is becoming part of basic search visibility.

    2026
  • Compliance
    GST e-invoice portals now reject invoices older than 30 days

    Businesses above ₹10 crore turnover must report e-invoices within 30 days or risk rejection and ITC issues for their buyers — a timing discipline worth building into your invoicing process now.

    2026
  • Compliance
    Planned e-way bill changes paused, not cancelled

    A revision to the e-way bill system due August 2026 was put on hold rather than scrapped — logistics and dispatch teams should expect it to resurface, not treat the pause as final.

    2026
  • Automation
    ERP vendors are shifting from dashboards to agent-driven operations

    Several major ERP platforms are moving AI from a reporting add-on into the actual finance and operations workflow — worth watching before over-investing in dashboard-only reporting tools.

    2026
  • Automation
    Cross-platform workflow automation is now standard, not optional

    Native automation is now built into most major CRMs, with connector tools handling sync between CRM, ERP and everything else — “we'll connect it eventually” is no longer a reasonable plan.

    2026
  • Commerce
    India's quick-commerce market is set to multiply several times over by 2029

    Estimates range from $3.65bn to $6.94bn in 2026, projected toward $12–68bn by 2029–2031 — every estimate agrees on direction: a quick-commerce plan is no longer optional for physical-goods brands.

    2026
  • Commerce
    ONDC is emerging as the low-commission alternative to marketplaces

    With marketplace and quick-commerce commissions running 15–28% versus roughly 3% on ONDC, more D2C brands are treating it as a genuine channel worth evaluating.

    2026
